Reporting a problem

It helps a lot if you tell us:

  • which page you were on and what you did,
  • what you expected to happen, and what happened instead,
  • your phone or computer, and the browser you use.

A screenshot is even better. Your results stay on your own device, so we can't see or repair your saved data, but we can help you work out what went wrong.
